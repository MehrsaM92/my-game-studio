📖 How To Play

Basic tips and solutions for better gameplay.

GUIDE 01

Choose Your Weapon

Pick a weapon based on the way you want to play. Consider range, accuracy, mobility and damage before building your setup.

GUIDE 02

Build Your Loadout

Combine the weapon with attachments that support your playstyle instead of simply choosing the most popular attachment.

GUIDE 03

Improve Your Aim

Practice your aim and learn the recoil pattern of your preferred weapon. Consistency is more useful than constantly changing settings.

GUIDE 04

Learn The Map

Pay attention to common routes, cover locations and dangerous areas. Good positioning can make a major difference in a match.

GUIDE 05

Use Attachments Carefully

Attachments usually involve tradeoffs. Improving one stat may reduce another, so build your weapon around the role you want it to perform.

GUIDE 06

Practice Your Setup

After creating a loadout, spend time using it before changing everything. Give yourself enough time to understand its strengths and weaknesses.
